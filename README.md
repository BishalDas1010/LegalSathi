# ⚖️ LegalSathi

**LegalSathi** is an AI-powered legal assistant designed to help users understand Indian laws, constitutional rights, and legal procedures through natural conversations. By combining Large Language Models (LLMs) with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), LegalSathi provides responses grounded in official legal documents instead of relying only on the model's internal knowledge.

> **Know Your Rights. Powered by AI.**

---

## 🚀 Features

* AI-powered legal chatbot
* RAG-based answers using official Indian legal documents
* Constitution and legal rights explorer
* Upload and chat with legal documents (PDF, DOCX)
* Voice interaction (Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech)
* Multi-language support
* Source citations for every answer
* Complaint and legal notice drafting (Planned)
* Company Policy Assistant (Planned)

---

## 🏛️ Knowledge Base

LegalSathi can retrieve information from documents such as:

* Constitution of India
* Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
* Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)
* Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)
* Information Technology Act
* Consumer Protection Act
* Motor Vehicles Act
* Right to Information (RTI) Act
* Labour Laws
* Government Notifications
* Company Policies & Employee Handbooks
